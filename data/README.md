# Data files

These are compact cleaned extracts used by the Vega/Vega-Lite charts.

- `instrument_waffle_6_1.csv` — derived 100-unit representation of ABS 6.1% instrument participation.
- `gss_creative_activities_2025.csv` — selected cultural activity participation percentages, ABS GSS 2025.
- `gss_instrument_by_age_2025.csv` — instrument participation by age, ABS GSS 2025.
- `gss_instrument_by_gender_2025.csv` — instrument participation by gender, ABS GSS 2025.
- `education_exposure_2025.csv` — school-age arts education comparison, Creative Australia NAPS 2025.
- `music_behaviour_by_age_2025.csv` — music creation/listening behaviour by age, Creative Australia NAPS 2025.
- `state_music_2025.csv` — state/territory music measures, Creative Australia NAPS 2025.
- `conservatorium_grants_2025_26.csv` — NSW Regional Conservatorium Grants Program approvals and approximate locality coordinates.
- `ameb_trend_2023_24_2024_25.csv` — AMEB NSW examination totals cited in the NESA 2024–25 annual report.
- `ameb_delivery_2024_25.csv` — AMEB NSW practical assessment delivery modes.
- `ameb_practical_formats_2024_25.csv` / `ameb_treemap_2024_25.csv` — practical music examination formats.
- `ameb_summary_2024_25.csv` — supporting AMEB NSW totals.

The page explicitly keeps ABS “singing or playing a musical instrument” separate from Creative Australia “create music”, because the survey measures are not equivalent.
